Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

This policy describes what TRUE STACK Poker ("the app") collects, why, and how you can request it be deleted. It applies to the app and its companion backend service.

Data we collect

  • Account identity: the username you choose, an internal player ID, your password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text), and a session token used to keep you signed in.
  • Gameplay data: table actions, hand history, timing, achievements, and high-hand highlights, used to run the game and fair-play verification.
  • Trust and safety signals: verification status, anti-cheat flags, and moderation records, used to protect the integrity of games.

Why we use it

  • To operate your account and restore your session securely across app restarts.
  • To run the poker games themselves, including fair-play review and replay verification of completed hands.
  • To support optional sharing of rare-hand achievements you choose to share.
  • To detect and act on abuse, collusion, or bot-like behavior.

What we don't do

  • We do not implement third-party ad tracking or sell your data.
  • Real-money payment processing, cash deposits, and cash withdrawals are not active in the current build. Any future real-money mode would require separate licensing, regulatory approval, and its own disclosures before it is enabled.
  • Sensitive moderation tooling is not exposed to end users.

Data retention

Account records (username, password hash, session tokens) are retained for as long as your account exists. Completed-hand records are retained as part of the fair-play ledger so that any player at a table can have a hand independently verified, in the same way for every player — this is retained even after an account is deleted, associated only with your internal player ID rather than your username.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account at any time from Profile → Account → Delete account inside the app. Deleting your account immediately removes your username and password, and signs your account out on every device. This action cannot be undone.
